Where we hope this is going

To be joined together, in Him

"For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined unto his wife, and they two shall be one flesh. This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church."Ephesians 5:31–32

We don't mean that as a figure of speech. We mean it plainly — God willing, our destination is to be united to each other, by and with Christ Himself, in the Church, through the Mystery of Matrimony. Not symbolically. Really.

"What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder."Matthew 19:6

That's why the Church can never undo a marriage crowned within her — how could anyone come between what God Himself has joined by the Holy Spirit, in such a mystery? Saint John Chrysostom preached that a marriage lived this way isn't an ordinary bond between two people, but something closer to a small church of its own — a place where two are drawn toward God together.

There's still work ahead of us before we're ready for that — some of it mine alone, some of it yours alone, and some of it ours together. We know what most of it is.
